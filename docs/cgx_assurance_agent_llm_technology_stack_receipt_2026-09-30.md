## Method separation

The implemented architecture preserves these distinctions:

- FMEA/FMECA: item/process/interface failure mechanisms and criticality. RPN is optional/local prioritisation, not a universal safety threshold.
- MPL: regulated financial/insurance consequence method where applicable; not physical mission-safety probability or technical readiness.
- Hazard analysis / HAZOP / FTA / ETA / BowTie / STPA: separate hazard, process-deviation, causal, event/barrier and system-control lenses.
- Functional safety: safety functions, independent controls and safe states.
- Cyber/AI assurance: model/tool/agent/memory/identity/privilege/supply-chain and adversarial risk.
- Eco/biosecurity: living-system, provenance, movement, pathogen/invasive and cumulative ecological pathways.
- Space debris / planetary protection: orbital-environment and contamination-specific gates.
- Assurance case: bounded claim/argument/evidence/defeater structure; never self-acceptance of residual risk.
- CGP-IES: affected-party, option-space, lifecycle, inheritance/contact and custodial reasoning; not the engineering safety kernel.
