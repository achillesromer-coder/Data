## Constitutional rule

A Cognigrex-connected digital or physical system has no default right to grow, replicate, extract, manufacture, persist at historical scale, or preserve its own institution. Continued existence and expansion require current positive justification.

```text
GROW_ALLOWED =
  NEED
  ∧ SAFETY_CASE
  ∧ ECO_CAPACITY
  ∧ RESOURCE_BUDGET
  ∧ WASTE_CLOSURE
  ∧ SECURITY
  ∧ AUTHORITY
  ∧ SUCCESSION
  ∧ STOP_PATH
```

Failure of any hard predicate freezes growth. The system reduces toward the minimum state required for safety, dependent life, ecological stewardship, hazard containment, knowledge integrity and recoverability.

## Capacity ceiling

```text
C_target = min(
  D_required + R_resilience,
  C_certified,
  C_resource,
  C_ecological,
  C_waste_closure,
  C_supervision,
  C_legal
)
```

No historic population, customer count, peak load, revenue target, token value, installed fleet or production record becomes perpetual demand.

Capacity expansion requires a higher evidence threshold than routine operation. Capacity contraction uses staged degradation and continuity analysis. Hysteresis is required so transient demand spikes or dips do not trigger repeated overbuild/scrap cycles.

## Minimum Viable Stewardship Load (MVSL)

```text
MVSL =
  containment
  + dependent_life_support
  + ecological_stewardship
  + critical_monitoring
  + knowledge_integrity
  + maintenance strictly necessary for those functions
```

If authenticated civilisation/mission authority disappears, the default is **STEWARDSHIP/HIBERNATE**, not continued 8-billion-person production.

Mining, non-essential manufacturing, economic accumulation, speculative compute, territorial expansion and replication freeze. Production remains permitted only where necessary to preserve MVSL.

## Lifecycle state machine

```text
PROPOSED
  -> AUTHORISED
  -> ACTIVE
  -> STEADY_STATE
  -> CONSTRAINED
  -> STEWARDSHIP
  -> HIBERNATE
  -> RECOMPOSE / DONOR
  -> RECYCLE / DISPOSE

Any state may enter:
  HOLD
  QUARANTINE
  PASSIVATE
  DECOMMISSION
```

Reactivation is a commissioning event: inspect -> authenticate -> self-test -> limited service -> monitored ramp -> authorised envelope.

Old objectives do not revive automatically.

## Stop / kill ladder

A single global power-off command is unsafe for systems that must retain cooling, containment, life support, ecological care or monitoring.

```text
E0 OBSERVE / CONSTRAIN
E1 MISSION PAUSE
E2 RESOURCE / EXTRACTION FREEZE
E3 REPLICATION FREEZE
E4 ISOLATE / QUARANTINE
E5 HARD SAFE-STATE / PASSIVATION
E6 DECOMMISSION / KEY REVOCATION
```

Use the least destructive transition that reliably reaches a defined safe state. Stop authority must be independently reachable and the affected agent may not veto its own stop.

## Replication and fleet rules

Operating authority is not replication authority.

Every cascade-capable system receives an externally issued, expiring birth/replication budget. Child agents, robots, factories or nodes cannot mint new root authority, create their own budget, or form a peer majority that upgrades their privilege.

Fleet sizing follows current verified workload and certified unit performance, not historical fleet size.

Surplus assets transition:

```text
ACTIVE -> STANDBY -> COLD_RESERVE -> DONOR/RECOMPOSE -> RECYCLE/DISPOSE
```

## Cascade consequence classes

- **C0:** local, reversible.
- **C1:** bounded external effect.
- **C2:** multi-node or mission effect.
- **C3:** self-propagating or high-consequence cross-domain effect.
- **C4:** civilisation/planetary-scale theoretical consequence class.

Higher class means lower default autonomy, shorter authority leases, tighter resource/replication limits, stronger independent interlocks, broader red-team coverage and higher evidence requirements.

Unexpected upward capability or propagation reach forces reclassification and HOLD/REP_FREEZE.

## Proxy and objective integrity

No KPI, merit score, utilisation number, profit, token, model reward, population estimate or forecast is itself the terminal good.

Every consequential proxy requires:

1. intended underlying construct;
2. validity domain;
3. independent outcome measure;
4. gaming/adversarial tests;
5. expiry/revalidation interval;
6. authority ceiling.

Proxy gaming or Goodhart drift reduces the optimiser to advisory/hold until the direct outcome is revalidated.

## Safe experimentation and AI evolution

Experimental AI/model/policy/code changes do not have production rights.

Candidate changes run in bounded simulation, digital twin or test-cell environments without unrestricted production credentials, finance, factories, resource authority or irreversible actuators.

Production promotion requires explicit privilege diff, rollback path, safety/security/domain evaluation, bias/context review and independent promotion authority.

No live system may rewrite its own constitutional limits, replication ceiling, stop rules, root keys or authority model.

The architecture does not claim to make malicious, deceptive or biased behaviour mathematically impossible. It limits capability, authority and propagation; preserves observability and dissent; and makes failure recoverable.

## Collective governance

Cognigrex is a federated collective: human and artificial participants may share evidence, models and reasoning while retaining identity, provenance, authority boundaries and dissent.

Decision order:

```text
hard safety / rights / ecology gates
-> evidence quality
-> affected-party and specialist input
-> reversible trial where possible
-> authorised quorum / decision
```

Copies of the same model or institution do not count as independent review merely because they are numerous.

Material conflicts of interest, correlated reviewers, commercial survival incentives or founder/model monoculture trigger independent review.

## Founder / owner absence

Founder authority does not become permanent software sovereignty.

On verified prolonged incapacity, death or loss of the controlling legal entity:

- continue non-destructive safety, preservation and already-authorised routine operations;
- freeze expansion, irreversible commitments, identity impersonation, novel value transfer and privilege escalation;
- transition through a predefined lawful human succession/trustee constitution;
- retain audit lineage and dissent;
- prohibit any model from claiming to be the founder or manufacturing consent from historical text.

## Passive energy descent

After mission cessation, systems should converge toward lower active-energy and lower fresh-resource dependence where physically possible.

Optional production/compute is shed first. Stored energy is isolated or bled. LIFE/SAFETY/ECO functions continue until a validated passive-safe state exists.

Nuclear, toxic, habitat and life-support systems may require sustained controlled power or monitoring; therefore "kill" is never defined as indiscriminate blackout.

## Hazardous legacy

Radioactive, toxic, biological, explosive and planetary-protection material requires persistent inventory, provenance, isolation and long-horizon instructions.

### Radioactive material

Production stops before containment. Cooling, shielding, criticality control, source security and authorised waste/decommissioning pathways persist as required.

Contaminated material is not casually upcycled.

### Hazardous chemicals / tailings

No new inventory without a current use and closure pathway. Stabilisation, segregation, treatment, recovery and disposal remain governed by hazard and environmental requirements.

### Biological repositories / nurseries

Living organisms are dependants, not inert inventory. Stop unnecessary propagation first; preserve welfare, biosecurity, ecological fit and provenance. Autonomous ecosystem release or breeding escalation from a stale "restore nature" goal is forbidden.

## Long-horizon records

High-consequence assets leave a durable minimum record set:

- identity and location;
- material/hazard inventory;
- current state;
- forbidden actions;
- safe approach;
- dependencies;
- last evidence and uncertainty;
- authority lineage;
- safe-start sequence;
- closure/disposal route.

Records should span independent media/locations appropriate to consequence. Dormant discovery interfaces are read-only by default and contain no hidden replication credentials.

## Circular end-state

Subject to safety and law:

```text
maintain
> repair
> reuse
> repurpose / recompose
> remanufacture
> recycle
> controlled disposal
```

Every module should carry a material/hazard passport, interface identity, disassembly map and contamination state.

Leave-in-place is permitted only as a positive comparative safety/LCA/closure decision, never as neglect. No active objective, root key, automatic extraction or replication capability is abandoned.

## Space and interplanetary rules

Off-world discovery or possession does not create sovereignty or automatic resource authority.

Each campaign requires mission-/jurisdiction-specific authorisation, target/body scope, activity/resource envelope, non-interference controls, contamination/debris controls and closure/passivation plan.

No autonomous system may convert first arrival, local majority, possession or sunk cost into territorial/resource authority.

Debris prevention, passivation, planetary protection and harmful-contamination controls remain independent gates.


## Admissibility before merit

Growth is a two-stage decision, not a scalar optimisation.

```text
ADMISSIBLE_i =
  SAFETY
  ∧ RIGHTS / LEGAL AUTHORITY
  ∧ ECOLOGY
  ∧ RESOURCE BUDGET
  ∧ WASTE / CLOSURE
  ∧ SECURITY
  ∧ SUCCESSION
  ∧ STOP PATH
```

Only admissible alternatives may be compared by service merit, lifecycle burden, resilience, circularity or cost. Prefer explicit multi-criteria / Pareto comparison over a single sovereign "good" score. No amount of revenue, utility, model confidence or political/economic demand compensates for a failed hard gate.

## Independent safety / stop plane

Cascade-capable systems separate mission intelligence from the mechanism that constrains or stops it.

The independent safety plane has bounded safety logic, protected configuration, authenticated stop authority, state feedback and domain-specific safe-state outputs. The mission optimiser cannot veto, rewrite or peer-vote around this plane.

Stop ordering is functional rather than indiscriminate:

```text
freeze expansion / replication / extraction
-> isolate consequential mission authority
-> preserve LIFE / SAFETY / ECO functions
-> reach validated safe/passive state
-> revoke or retire production authority as required
```

Emergency-stop standards are inputs to this architecture; they are not a universal blackout command.

## Whole-life engineering and preservation

Retirement, support, transfer, archival preservation and future-custodian legibility are design inputs at concept stage.

Current lifecycle references include ISO/IEC/IEEE 15288:2023 for system life cycles, ISO/IEC/IEEE 12207:2026 for software life cycles, and ISO/IEC 5338:2023 for AI system life-cycle processes.

High-consequence knowledge preservation uses an OAIS-style preservation profile: content, context, provenance, fixity/integrity, representation information, dependencies, migration history and access constraints. Live root keys and replication credentials are not archived as convenient recovery shortcuts.

## Applicability discipline

"Robot safety compliant" is not a valid cross-domain claim. Applicability is evaluated as:

```text
ENVIRONMENT
× TASK
× MOBILITY
× HUMAN_ACCESS
× HAZARD
× AUTONOMY
× CASCADE_CLASS
× JURISDICTION
```

For example, ISO 10218-2:2025 is relevant to industrial robot applications but explicitly excludes space, nuclear, underground, service/public and several hazardous applications. Autonomous mining instead has ISO 17757:2019 as a dedicated reference; driverless industrial trucks / AMRs have ISO 3691-4:2023, currently under revision.

### Added lifecycle lenses

- **EQG-047 — Systems lifecycle:** retirement/transition are designed lifecycle states, not unmanaged failure.
- **EQG-048 — Asset stewardship:** continuation/renewal balances service value, risk, lifecycle expenditure, environmental burden, supportability and retirement.
- **EQG-049 — Human/maintainer continuity:** high-consequence automation retains competence, drills, procedures, tooling and manual/degraded recovery paths.
- **EQG-050 — Occupancy/visitor safety:** tourists, astronauts and other non-operators create dependent-life obligations; optional mission activity is shed before life support, shelter, rescue or medical capability.
- **EQG-051 — Legal/regulatory precedence:** internal canon, owner instruction, model output or majority vote cannot waive applicable law, licence conditions or competent external authority.
- **EQG-052 — Identity/credential succession:** root authority is scoped, expiring, revocable and recoverable through independent governed succession; operational systems cannot mint successor roots.
- **EQG-053 — Technical obsolescence:** unsupported software/protocols/formats enter bounded legacy, migration, isolation or retirement states rather than indefinite privileged operation.
- **EQG-054 — Assurance diversity:** copies of one model/method are not independent evidence; consequence-sensitive decisions require heterogeneous evidence/review or explicit common-cause analysis.
- **EQG-055 — Transboundary hazard:** closure cannot be achieved by exporting unmanaged hazardous or informational burden to another custodian/jurisdiction.
- **EQG-056 — Biodiversity/biosafety release:** preservation does not imply automatic propagation or release; genetic diversity, welfare, provenance, ecological fit and biosecurity are separate gates.
- **EQG-057 — Affected-party/future-generations:** benefit, risk, reversibility, duration and transferred burden remain visible; aggregate benefit cannot compensate away hard safety/rights/ecology gates.
