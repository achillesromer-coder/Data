# Post-Mix Programme Interaction Map v0.1

**Canonical control:** PMX-GRAPH-001  
**Status:** additive systems integration; evidence ceilings preserved.

The post-mix lens is not a new standalone programme. It is a control primitive that connects existing programmes through a common **material passport → recipe → process → verification → reuse** architecture.

## End-to-end operating loop

1. **Need:** part, repair, infrastructure or scientific demand is defined.
2. **Capability query:** Cognigrex identifies permitted local/recycled stock, process cells and external shared capacity.
3. **Resource gate:** use existing/recycled stock first where safe; virgin extraction requires explicit resource, stewardship and rights basis.
4. **Characterise:** assay, PSD, contamination, volatiles and response properties.
5. **Beneficiate:** conventional baseline first; RFS/EMFF only as bounded experimental alternatives until evidence promotes them.
6. **Refine and passport:** produce qualified stock bins with uncertainty.
7. **Recipe:** solve non-negative feed rates to a target pre-process composition.
8. **Process:** apply process-specific thermal/chemical/metallurgical physics.
9. **Verify:** chemistry, microstructure, NDE, dimensions and required mechanical/electrical/PV evidence.
10. **Deploy:** only to the part class allowed by the acceptance record.
11. **Recover:** rejected/retired material re-enters characterisation as secondary feed.
12. **Learn:** actual yields, losses, failure modes and resource externalities update the graph.

## Why this expands Römer without becoming “Buy n Large”

Römer’s role should be **research → integration → orchestration → qualified operations**, with shared capacity and interoperable standards where those are safer or more efficient than ownership.

The strategic moat becomes the quality of the **material/process knowledge graph, evidence discipline, logistics integration, recipe control and mission coordination**, not compulsory ownership of every mine, refinery, printer, power system or settlement service.

The architecture therefore prefers:
- supplier enablement and shared industrial commons;
- qualified third-party process cells;
- open interchange schemas;
- local safe-state autonomy;
- independent assurance;
- recycle-first resource planning;
- explicit ecological/interspecies and planetary-protection gates;
- auditable benefit/externality accounting.

This is compatible with a large interplanetary enterprise while structurally resisting a closed monopoly.

## Evidence boundary

Mass-balance and multi-feed control are implemented as deterministic digital logic. Multi-material DED and NASA ISRU work are evidence-backed analogues. RFS/EMFF performance, Solar Hull material compatibility, mission-qualified recycled parts, autonomous asteroid-to-part operation and resource-rights viability remain open empirical/legal programme gates.
