# Distributed Interplanetary Enterprise Stewardship Guardrails v0.1

This control note exists so a self-sustaining resource/manufacturing network does not optimise itself into a closed, extractive, single-operator monopoly.

## Scope

Applies to resource characterisation, extraction, beneficiation, feedstock qualification, recipe control, manufacturing, repair, recycling, infrastructure and Cognigrex/LightSpeed operational coordination.

## Hard constraints

- Safety, biological/ecological protection and planetary-protection limits are not tradable against cost or throughput.
- Internal registries do not create sovereignty, celestial ownership, mining rights, regulatory approval or public title.
- Resource extraction requires an explicit authority/basis appropriate to location and mission.
- High-consequence or irreversible actions require accountable human/independent review.
- Recycle/reuse and lower-impact sourcing are evaluated before virgin extraction.
- Preserve/no-touch zones and scientific-value constraints must be representable in the planning graph.
- No feedstock recipe becomes an engineering material specification without assay, process qualification and acceptance testing.
- RFS/EMFF remain experimental modules until performance is measured.

## Anti-concentration architecture

1. Open/interoperable material-passport and recipe schemas.
2. Exportable data and non-proprietary interchange formats.
3. Third-party qualification pathway for tools, habitats, vehicles and manufacturers.
4. Local safe-state operation without central Römer/Cognigrex connectivity.
5. Independent assurance and audit separated from execution.
6. Material/resource ledgers record mass balance, losses, rejected builds and externalities.
7. Capability planning optimises minimum necessary extraction, not maximum throughput.
8. Economic optimisation includes resilience, repairability, recyclability and shared infrastructure.
9. Benefit-sharing and scientific/public-interest outputs are explicit objectives.
10. Right-to-exit/fork: no node is technically forced to remain inside one commercial control plane.

## Interspecial egalitarian interpretation

Where human activity can affect biological systems, species, habitats or potentially living environments, welfare and habitat constraints enter as hard planning limits before commercial optimisation. Unknown biological status is handled through precautionary classification rather than assumed absence of value.

## Operational separation

Recommended role split:

- EMASSC / IP custody and licensing.
- Römer / engineering integration and operations.
- Cognigrex / decision support, provenance, simulation and orchestration.
- Independent verification / engineering, safety, ecological and legal review.
- Local operators / node autonomy and safe-state authority.
- Public-interest / scientific stewardship review for irreversible or high-externality actions.

No single role should self-author, self-approve, execute and certify the same high-consequence resource action.

## Programme objective

Build a federated industrial ecology capable of repairing and reproducing useful infrastructure from local and recycled resources, while preserving interoperability, scientific value, ecological/biological safeguards and accountable external review.
